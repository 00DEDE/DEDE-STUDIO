Happiness 003

Analog
Daydreaming

01Overview

If I can touch it, I can feel it.

To find peace, I go back to pencil and paper. Sometimes I venture out to chalk or markers. I think with my hands. If I can touch it, I can feel it. And if I can feel it, I will bond with it.

Over the past couple of years, I have built a habit of drawing the names of people I care about and giving them as gifts. On my last day at Hulu, I took the time to draw a large-scale art piece on a blank blackboard in the office. It has always been a dream of mine to one day create a mural of my own, and that piece at Hulu was the first time I felt close to that dream.

This is my solitude. This is my peace. Creativity in free flow. No mental blocks, no doubts, just the flow of hand and mind.
