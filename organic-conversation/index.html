01Overview

Understanding space and proportion in organic forms.

To me, organic material is sacred. Life runs through it. I especially love working with raw vegetable-tanned leather and wood. In a world constantly pushing the intangible and digital, being able to experience life by creating in the tangible and physical excites me.

I get goosebumps thinking of the textures unique to each piece of wood and leather. No piece is the same as the next. Each is ever-changing and finite, like you and me. This is the beauty of organic materials: they began as life long before human hands ever touched them.
