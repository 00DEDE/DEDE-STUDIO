Happiness 001

Hulu
Experience

01Overview

Hey Hulu? Thank you!

I was blessed with the privilege of interning on Hulu’s creative team for seven months. It was my first experience working alongside industry professionals and learning from them. The team trusted me and valued my input, and that meant so much to me.

I brought forward ideas and concepts for movies and shows that have since been released. I was part of the creative process for Hulu’s Hispanic Heritage Month campaign. I designed merchandise for Hulu and Disney teams, real merchandise that is out in the world today.

My proudest moment: I designed the logo and logo animation for TMRWLAND, an internal event hosted on a Disney lot that brought together the creative teams across Disney Entertainment Television. More than 300 people attended, and the merchandise given out that day carried the logo I made.

Thank you to Reid, Heather, and Matt for your trust in me. Thank you to the whole Hulu Creative Team for making me feel welcome and valued. What an amazing experience it was to work with so many talented people.
