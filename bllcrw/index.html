01Overview

Late Nights. Early Mornings. BC.

A lifelong project. A goal I am living in and still dreaming of. BLLCRW. (pronounced Ball Crew).

This is what started my journey in graphic design. I went to my local community college to take a class in Photoshop and Illustrator so I could prepare print-ready files for our manufacturers. Along the way, I fell in love with graphic design. I went on to earn an associate degree in graphic design and another in art history. My portfolio for ArtCenter was 90% BLLCRW. It has taken me places I never would have imagined.

The brand is still in its infancy, because this is a generational dream. My goal is for it to outlive me, for this tree I planted with my team to bear fruit long after I am gone. None of this would be possible without them. They have believed in my dreams, as I have believed in theirs.

Owning a clothing company has taught me to communicate clearly, delegate responsibilities, and take calculated risks.

BC this. BC that.
