Case Study · 006

Café
Bustelo

01Overview

In 2028, Café Bustelo turns 100, and a milestone like that deserves a celebration.

Café Bustelo started in East Harlem, where Gregorio and Angelina Bustelo hand-ground coffee in their home. Beginning in 1928, by 1931, he had opened his first storefront on Fifth Avenue. For nearly 100 years, the brand has been in the homes of millions. To Latin communities, Café Bustelo is much more than a can of coffee. It stands for our goals, our dreams, and our perseverance.

That’s what I wanted to carry forward. I set out to build a new system for Café Bustelo: one unified story, celebratory but poised, true to its roots while pushing them into the next century. One question guided the work: we’ve seen what Café Bustelo’s first 100 years looked like, so what does the bridge between its past and its future look like?

For me, the answer was always in the grassroots: our communities back home. This refreshed Café Bustelo keeps the same bold coffee, now inside a world that pays homage to the ones who started it all. This project began as a coffee rebrand and became much more. This one is for my people. You sacrificed so much for greener lands. Thank you.
