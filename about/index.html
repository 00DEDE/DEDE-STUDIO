About Me

I did not speak until I was six years old. My first attempts were simple: “Day... Day...” My mother writes it as “Dede.”

Before words, I communicated with my hands, and this has never left me. Today, I think, feel, and tell stories through creating work that exists between both the digital and the analog. I trace connections between people, places, and ideas the way a child might trace lines in the sand, until their own world begins to emerge.

I am David Gomez. In closer company, I am Dede. It’s good to meet you. Let’s make today... a day where you and I create something meaningful.
